FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions local business owners ask me most often.

How long does it take to see results from local SEO?

+

Most clients see measurable improvements in Google Maps rankings and organic traffic within 60–90 days. Full results — including consistent top 3 Maps positions — typically emerge within 90–120 days. It's worth noting that in highly competitive markets, achieving dominant local rankings can take anywhere from 3 to 6 months. The timeline depends on your starting point, your local market's competition level and the services involved — I always set honest, clear milestone expectations before we begin.

What makes local SEO different from regular SEO?

+

Local SEO is specifically focused on ranking your business in geographically relevant searches — particularly in Google Maps and the Local 3-Pack. It involves unique signals like Google Business Profile optimization, NAP citation consistency, local review management and proximity-based ranking factors that don't apply to traditional SEO.

Do I need a website to benefit from local SEO?

+

Not necessarily, but having a well-optimized website significantly amplifies your local SEO results. Your Google Business Profile can rank in Maps without a website, but a properly optimized site adds powerful ranking signals — location pages, schema markup, local content — that a GBP listing alone cannot provide. I'll advise you on the best approach for your situation.

How much do your local SEO services cost?

+

Pricing varies depending on the scope of work, your market's competitiveness and which services you need. I offer flexible, month-to-month engagements with no long-term contracts. The best first step is a free audit — I'll analyze your business and provide a clear recommendation with transparent pricing before you commit to anything.

Can you rank my business in a city where I don't have a physical address?

+

This is one of the trickiest areas of local SEO. Google strongly prefers businesses with a verified physical address in the target city. For service-area businesses, there are legitimate strategies to expand visibility — service area settings, content optimization, link building and citations — but I will always give you an honest assessment of what's realistically achievable for your situation.

What reporting will I receive?

+

You'll receive detailed monthly reports covering ranking changes, traffic growth, GBP insights (calls, direction requests, views), review activity and the work completed — you're always fully in the loop.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings?

+

No ethical SEO professional can guarantee specific rankings — Google's algorithm is complex and ultimately out of anyone's control. What I do guarantee is transparent, proven work using white-hat methods, clear communication and a relentless focus on moving your rankings in the right direction. My 97% client retention rate reflects the quality of results I consistently deliver.
